Hello! I am Muhammad Waseem, a Ph.D. Candidate in the [Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering](https://engineering.virginia.edu/departments/mechanical-and-aerospace-engineering) at the [University of Virginia (UVA)](https://www.virginia.edu/). I am currently being advised by Prof. [Qing (Cindy) Chang](https://engineering.virginia.edu/faculty/qing-cindy-chang/). My primary research focus lies in the broad spectrum of Complex Systems Modelling and Control. 



# Research interests:
My research focuses on the automation and control of complex systems. I explore the application of generative AI in production planning and control, aiming to enhance adaptability and efficiency in manufacturing environments. I have hands-on experience in modeling and controlling smart manufacturing systems, and I actively apply machine learning and deep reinforcement learning techniques to develop intelligent, data-driven solutions for industrial challenges.
